What systeme.io actually is

systeme.io is an all-in-one platform for running a small online business. One login covers:

  • Sales funnels and opt-in (lead capture) pages
  • Email marketing: broadcasts, newsletters, and automated sequences
  • Automation rules and workflows (tagging, triggers)
  • Online courses and communities
  • Websites and a blog
  • Checkout with Stripe or PayPal, order bumps, and 1-click upsells
  • Running your own affiliate program for your products
  • Booking calendar and a link-in-bio page

The trade-off is breadth over depth. Each piece is simpler than the best dedicated tool for that job, but it all works together without integrations.
