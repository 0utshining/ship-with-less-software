About

About Anthony DeToma

I run Ship With Less Software, an independent site that helps creators, freelancers, and solopreneurs choose AI tools that pay for themselves and skip the ones that don't.

Why this site exists

Most "best AI tools" lists just pile up logos. People who publish or sell online every week need something else: one dependable tool per job, a hard monthly budget, and a straight answer about when the free tier is enough. That's what I write.

Who it's for

  • Creators publishing newsletters, videos, or blogs on a schedule
  • Freelancers who deliver copy, content, or media to clients
  • Solopreneurs running a one-person offer: a course, a service, or a digital product

It's not written for enterprise IT buyers or for people who collect demos.

How I review tools (editorial standards)

  1. Job first. Each tool gets judged on one job (write, rank, design, voice, video, email, or ops) and compared with the free baseline for that job.
  2. Hands-on where possible. I set up an account and run a real task. If a section relies on vendor documentation instead of my own use, I say so.
  3. Pricing gets dated. I check prices on the vendor's official page and note the date. I don't copy numbers from roundup sites.
  4. Everything has a skip case. Every review says who should not buy.
  5. Affiliate status never decides a verdict. I recommend tools I don't earn from, and I tell readers to stay on free tools when that's the right call.
  6. Original work. Checklists, worksheets, and reviews are written from scratch. No republished third-party prompt packs.
